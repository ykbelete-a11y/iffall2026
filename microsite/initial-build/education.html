Education & Moving

Each move gave Suh a new home to remember and a new way of working. A sewing class in Rhode Island turned a painter into a sculptor.

The cities he has called home

  1. 1980s

    Seoul National University

    Seoul, South Korea

    Earns a BFA and MFA in Oriental painting, learning the ink traditions his father practiced.

  2. Late 1980s

    Military service

    South Korea

    Serves his required time in the Korean military, an experience of uniforms and group identity that returns in his work.

  3. 1991

    Rhode Island School of Design

    Providence, Rhode Island

    Moves to the US. Pattern-making classes teach him to sew, and he begins shifting from painting toward sculpture.

  4. 1997

    MFA in Sculpture, Yale University

    New Haven, Connecticut

    Completes his graduate degree. Fabric becomes his main material.

  5. 1997–2010

    A decade in New York

    New York City

    Lives at 348 West 22nd Street, a home he would later recreate room by room.

  6. 2010

    Move to London

    London, United Kingdom

    Settles in London, adding another home, and another set of doors and hallways, to his work.
