Exhibitions & recognition

Exhibitions

From the Venice Biennale to Tate Modern, Suh's fabric houses have been shown around the world.

2001

49th Venice Biennale

Suh represented South Korea at the 2001 Venice Biennale, one of the most important international art events, bringing his work to a worldwide audience.

2017

Ho-Am Prize in the Arts

One of South Korea's most respected awards, recognizing his contribution to the arts.

Where his work lives

His work is held in public collections including the Museum of Modern Art and the Solomon R. Guggenheim Museum in New York and Tate Modern in London.

How critics read the work

Critics often describe Suh as an artist of the global, moving life. They see his see-through houses as images of how people who live between countries hold on to memory.
